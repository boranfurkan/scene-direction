# Critique: look at it like a juror

Run the check (it writes the screenshots and scroll films). Then look at `desktop.png`, `phone.png`, `film-desktop.png` and `film-phone.png` yourself, and score each line 1–5. Then **fix the three weakest things, check, and look again: up to three rounds.** Fix defects with the smallest change that keeps the design. Fix weak scores (drama, atmosphere, variety) by adding substance, not by removing things.

Before scoring, ask the juror's question: *if these screenshots arrived next to a rival build of the same brief, would this one win?* Judges weigh the overall experience. A striking site with a small flaw beats a safe site with none, but a broken, blank or unreadable site loses.

| | Question | 1 looks like | 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Idea** | Can you say the idea sentence, and does it fail the competitor test? | an effect (orb, particles, globe) standing in for an idea | the 3D could only belong to this subject |
| **Signature** | Does the first screen show the one thing a visitor will remember? | a generic hero with the good part three scrolls down | the object at its best, on the first frame, with the name |
| **Colour world** | Does the 3D share the page's colours, and do scenes change the world? | grey object, black void, one colour for the whole page | object, light and ground are one palette that moves with the story |
| **Object craft** | Silhouette, material, details: does it look made? | sharp primitives, uniform grey, no detail | real proportions, bevels, believable material, details in the site's own type |
| **Light & finish** | Is there shape, separation and grounding, and is the frame graded? | flat, blown out or murky; floating; a raw render | key, fill and rim that model the form; contact shadows and AO; a source that glows; a vignette that holds the eye |
| **Story & motion** | Does each scene have a beat, and does the camera mean something? | spinning, aimless, the same view everywhere | entrance-hold-exit; each move shows something new |
| **Drama & scale** | Is the first screen striking, and does the object or world own the frame? | a small object in an empty frame, a safe split layout | full-bleed, a big hero, type at display size, a shot you'd screenshot |
| **Atmosphere & world** | Is there a place, a time of day, a light inside the scene? | an object on a void, even light, no mood | a world that belongs to the subject: a room, a table, a landscape, fog, a lit window |
| **Type & copy** | Real content, clear hierarchy, type with character? | placeholder copy, one size of grey text | the subject's real words, a display face with a point of view |
| **Live thing** | Is there one interaction from the idea that works with a thumb? | none, or a toolbox of controls | one clear verb, and it's fun |
| **Site, not demo** | Header, index, footer, a short intro, real title and copy, native cursor? | a canvas with text on it | a site you could put live today: navigation, contact, a branded reveal under ~2 s |
| **Floor** | Is the check clean (or every remaining item explained)? | crashes, cropped on phones, unreadable text | nothing a visitor would see as broken |

Notes for scoring:
- Score from the screenshots, not from the code. If the film shows a frame you wouldn't screenshot and share, that scene isn't finished.
- A defect fix must not lower another score. Moving the copy off the object so the check passes, if it kills a composition that scored 5, is the wrong fix: put a scrim behind the text instead (legibility.md).
- Ambition is part of the score. A clean page with nothing memorable is a 2 on Idea and Signature, however tidy it is.
